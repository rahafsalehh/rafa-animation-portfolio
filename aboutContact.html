ABOUT

Hi I'm Rafa Saleh, an animator and visual storyteller, driven by how impactful stories and messages can be shown without the use of words, but visuals instead. Im 18 years old, I love drawing, gaming, anime and pizza. I grew up not speaking often though I always showcased things I wanted to say through art. Its my dream some day to make my very own indie show with my own original characters, though this is a dream im investing to do later on in the future. I specialise in 2d animation, character design; concept art, though I love stepping outside my comfort zone to try something new.
